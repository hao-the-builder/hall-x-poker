# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Residents of HKUST Hall X who play in the hall's regular poker nights. Everyone signs up with their `@connect.ust.hk` address; there are no outsiders. They use the site in three moments: on their phone at the table ("who's playing tonight? sign me up"), right after a game when the host records the finishing order, and idly during the week to check the leaderboard and argue about it in the group chat.

Two roles, one account type: any signed-in member can host a tournament (create it, enter results); everyone can join an open table and read everything.

## Product Purpose

A rating system for hall poker. It replaces a spreadsheet nobody trusts with a shared, verified record: who played, where they finished, how many points that earned, a rating built from the share of opponents beaten, and a letter tier from S to E. Success is that the leaderboard is the thing people screenshot into the group chat, and that nobody disputes how the number was produced because the site explains it.

Non-goals: no chips, buy-ins, money, or hand tracking. No real-time play. No public marketing page; this is a members-only tool.

## Brand Personality

Competitive but friendly. Three words: **precise, dry, sporting.** It takes the rankings seriously, the way a club noticeboard does, and leaves the trash talk to the players. Copy is short and confident, with a little wit in empty states and confirmations, never in the data.

Visual direction: **matte black and rose gold.** A private card room after hours, not a casino floor. Flat near-black ground with no gloss or glow, warm bone-white ink, one rose-gold accent that also colours the red suits. Playing-card typography and suit pips carry the theme, quietly.

## Anti-references

- Casino kitsch: green felt textures, gold gradients, chip and neon imagery, Vegas serif logos.
- Generic SaaS dashboard: stat-tile hero metrics, purple/blue gradients, cards nested in cards, Inter everywhere, uppercase tracked eyebrows over every section.
- Crypto/gaming "dark mode with glow" UI.
- Spreadsheet dumps with no hierarchy.

## Design Principles

1. **The leaderboard is the product.** Every other screen exists to feed it. It gets the best typography, the most care, and the fewest distractions.
2. **Show the working.** Ratings are never a black box: matches, points, and the formula are always one click away, and the numbers on screen are the numbers in the database.
3. **Fast at the table.** Joining a tournament and entering results must work one-handed on a phone in under a minute, with no modals and no surprises.
4. **On-theme through craft, not decoration.** Poker shows up in numerals, suits, and the rhythm of a printed sheet, not in textures or imagery.
5. **Earned familiarity.** Standard controls, standard navigation, consistent states. The tool should disappear into the task.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. Body text at or above 4.5:1, focus-visible rings on every control, 44px touch targets, full keyboard paths for join/leave/enter results. Suits are never the only carrier of meaning (rank numerals and text labels always accompany them). All motion respects `prefers-reduced-motion`. Works on a 360px-wide phone.
